# Open Core Scope

HiEnglishLab Diagnosis Framework uses an open-core model. This repository is the public evidence-processing foundation; it is not the complete validated or commercial diagnosis system.

## Included in the open-source core

- Versioned, vendor-neutral evidence and report schemas.
- Local CLI and Python interfaces for deterministic analysis.
- Basic, explainable descriptive metrics.
- Synthetic examples, tests, privacy safeguards, and threat models.
- Safe extension interfaces that do not execute untrusted plugin code.
- Community-maintained documentation and translations.

## Outside this repository

The following may be developed and maintained separately and are not promised as open-source components:

- Proprietary rubric weights, thresholds, and scoring calibration.
- Normative datasets, labelled learner corpora, and longitudinal benchmarks.
- Validated KET, PET, IELTS, or other exam-alignment packs.
- Personalised teaching prescriptions, curriculum sequencing, and progress models.
- Commercial reports, dashboards, integrations, operations, and support workflows.
- Private evaluation sets, prompts, red-team cases, and quality-control procedures.

## Exam and proficiency claims

The current open-source core does not issue CEFR levels, Cambridge English exam scores, IELTS bands, or equivalent credentials. Its metrics may support human review, but they are not substitutes for official tasks, trained examiners, validated scoring procedures, or complete assessment of reading, writing, listening, and speaking.

Any future public exam mapping must:

1. cite current official public sources;
2. distinguish observable evidence from inferred proficiency;
3. publish validation methods and limitations;
4. avoid reproducing copyrighted exam content or confidential scoring material; and
5. avoid implying endorsement or affiliation.

Cambridge, KET, PET, IELTS, and related marks belong to their respective owners. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge University Press & Assessment, the British Council, IDP, or IELTS partners.
